faq_title: Ornamental fence questions
faq:
  - q: "Can I put an ornamental fence on my golf course or lake lot in The Woodlands?"
    a: "Yes. Solid fences aren't allowed along the rear line of golf course and lake lots, so a see-through metal fence is the usual choice. It can be up to 6'6\" tall and needs RDRC approval like any new fence."
  - q: "Will an ornamental fence keep my dog in?"
    a: "For most dogs, yes. Small dogs can slip between standard pickets, so ask about puppy pickets. See [pet fences](/fence-styles/pet/)."
  - q: "Steel or aluminum?"
    a: "Both give the same look. Steel is heavier and stiffer, which helps on drive gates and street frontage. Aluminum can't rust, which helps around pools and water. See [ornamental steel](/materials/ornamental-steel-aluminum/) and [aluminum](/materials/aluminum/)."

    {% include eyebrow.html text="Local rules" %}
    <h2>Ornamental fence rules in The Woodlands and nearby</h2>
    <p>Section 2.6 of The Woodlands Township's <a href="https://www.thewoodlandstownship-tx.gov/files/sharedassets/main/v/2/covenant-administration/documents/2024-residential-standards-recorded-12-10-2024.pdf" rel="noopener">Residential Development Standards</a> treats ornamental metal as a transparent fence:</p>
    <ul class="fx-checklist">
      <li><span><b>Approval first.</b> Every new fence needs written approval from your village RDRC.</span></li>
      <li><span><b>Height.</b> Transparent fences top out at 6'6". Perimeter fences must be at least 4 feet tall.</span></li>
      <li><span><b>Golf course and lake lots.</b> Solid fences aren't allowed along the rear line, so ornamental is the usual choice.</span></li>
      <li><span><b>Front yards.</b> A transparent fence in a front yard has to sit at least 5 feet back from the front property line, with plants between it and the street.</span></li>
      <li><span><b>Gates and columns.</b> Along the front of the lot, decorative columns and gates are limited to 12 inches above the fence on lots under 85 feet wide, or 8 feet on wider lots.</span></li>
      <li><span><b>Mesh.</b> Black mesh up to 3 feet tall can go inside a wrought iron fence as a pet barrier for six months or less.</span></li>
    </ul>
    <p>Outside The Woodlands, lake and golf communities in Spring, Kingwood, and Atascocita often set similar rules for water and fairway lots, and your HOA usually sets the color. See <a href="{{ '/service-areas/the-woodlands/' | relative_url }}">fencing in The Woodlands</a>.</p>
    <p class="fx-note">Summary only. The recorded standards and your neighborhood's criteria control, and they can change.</p>
