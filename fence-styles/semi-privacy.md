faq_title: Semi-privacy fence questions
faq:
  - q: "How much can people see through a shadowbox fence?"
    a: "Straight on, very little. The boards on one side cover the gaps on the other. From an angle you can see slivers of the yard. If you need full screening, a board-on-board privacy fence is the better fit."
  - q: "Do I need my neighbor's permission for a shadowbox fence in The Woodlands?"
    a: "A shadowbox is finished on both sides, so it doesn't fall under the rule about a fence's unfinished side facing a neighbor. A good neighbor fence, built in alternating sections with the rails facing out, does need the adjacent owner's written consent. Every new fence still needs RDRC approval."
  - q: "How tall can a semi-privacy fence be in The Woodlands?"
    a: "Shadowbox and lattice-top fences are solid fences under the Standards, so the solid limits apply: 7'6\", or 8'6\" along designated major streets, plus 2 inches for a cap."
  - q: "Does a shadowbox fence cost more than a regular privacy fence?"
    a: "Usually a little. Boards go on both sides of the rails, so it takes more pickets and more labor per foot than a side-by-side fence."

    <h2>Semi-privacy fence rules in The Woodlands and nearby</h2>
    <p>Section 2.6 of The Woodlands Township's <a href="https://www.thewoodlandstownship-tx.gov/files/sharedassets/main/v/2/covenant-administration/documents/2024-residential-standards-recorded-12-10-2024.pdf" rel="noopener">Residential Development Standards</a> applies, and its fence design appendix includes shadowbox, capped shadowbox, and framed lattice designs:</p>
    <ul class="fx-checklist">
      <li><span><b>Approval first.</b> Every new fence needs written approval from your village RDRC.</span></li>
      <li><span><b>Counts as solid.</b> A fence less than 75 percent see-through is a solid fence. Solid fences top out at 7'6", or 8'6" along designated major streets, with 2 more inches for a cap.</span></li>
      <li><span><b>Placement.</b> Solid fences stay at least 3 feet behind the front of the house, and aren't allowed along the rear line of golf course and lake lots.</span></li>
      <li><span><b>Finished side out.</b> The finished side faces the street. A fence with its unfinished side toward a neighbor needs that neighbor's written consent.</span></li>
      <li><span><b>Good neighbor fences.</b> Alternating sections with the rails facing out need the adjacent owner's written consent. Repairing or replacing an existing one doesn't.</span></li>
    </ul>
    <p>Outside The Woodlands, your HOA's deed restrictions usually set height, style, and stain color. See <a href="{{ '/service-areas/the-woodlands/' | relative_url }}">fencing in The Woodlands</a>.</p>
    <p class="fx-note">Summary only. The recorded standards and your neighborhood's criteria control, and they can change.</p>
