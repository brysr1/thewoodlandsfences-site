faq:
  - q: "Can I build a dog run in The Woodlands?"
    a: "Yes, with limits. A dog run has to be in the back or side yard, at least 3 feet from a perimeter fence next to another home, and no taller than 6 feet or the height of the fence beside it. Chain link is allowed only if it's fully hidden from the street and neighbors by a solid fence. It needs approval like any other fence."
  - q: "Can I add mesh to my iron fence to keep a small dog in?"
    a: "In The Woodlands, black mesh up to 3 feet tall can go on the inside of a wrought iron fence for up to six months. For a permanent fix, steel with puppy pickets is the cleaner answer. Check your HOA rules elsewhere."
  - q: "How tall should a fence be for my dog?"
    a: "It depends on the dog. Most yards are fine at 6 feet. A determined jumper may need the most height your rules allow, which in The Woodlands is 7'6\" for a solid fence."

    <h2>Match the fence to the dog</h2>
    <ul class="fx-checklist">
      <li><span><b>Diggers.</b> Most escapes happen at the bottom. A tight bottom line and a dig barrier matter more than extra height.</span></li>
      <li><span><b>Jumpers and climbers.</b> Go taller, and avoid rails on the outside that a dog can use as a step.</span></li>
      <li><span><b>Barkers.</b> A solid wood fence blocks the view of squirrels, deer, and the neighbor's dog, which cuts down on barking.</span></li>
      <li><span><b>Small dogs.</b> Standard metal pickets can be wide enough to squeeze through. Puppy pickets close the gap at the bottom.</span></li>
      <li><span><b>Golf course and lake lots.</b> Solid fences aren't allowed along the back of these lots in The Woodlands, so steel with puppy pickets keeps the view and still holds the dog.</span></li>
    </ul>

    <h2>Pet fence rules in The Woodlands and nearby</h2>
    <p>In The Woodlands, pet fences follow Sections 2.4 and 2.6 of the Township's <a href="https://www.thewoodlandstownship-tx.gov/files/sharedassets/main/v/2/covenant-administration/documents/2024-residential-standards-recorded-12-10-2024.pdf" rel="noopener">Residential Development Standards</a>:</p>
    <ul class="fx-checklist">
      <li><span><b>Approval first.</b> A short interior fence is normally pre-approved, but not if it's used to hold an animal. Pet fences and dog runs need approval.</span></li>
      <li><span><b>Dog runs.</b> Back or side yard only, at least 3 feet from a perimeter fence next to another home, and no taller than 6 feet or the fence beside it. On lots with wrought iron fencing, the run has to be screened with plants.</span></li>
      <li><span><b>Chain link.</b> Allowed for a dog run only if a solid fence fully hides it from the street and neighbors.</span></li>
      <li><span><b>Mesh on iron fences.</b> Black mesh up to 3 feet tall, inside a wrought iron fence, for six months or less.</span></li>
      <li><span><b>Height.</b> Solid fences top out at 7'6" (8'6" along major streets); see-through fences at 6'6".</span></li>
    </ul>
    <p>Outside The Woodlands, your HOA's deed restrictions usually decide style and height. In Kingwood, the City of Houston requires a permit only for fences over 8 feet. In unincorporated Harris County, a residential fence outside the flood hazard area doesn't need a county permit. We check your address before we quote. See <a href="{{ '/service-areas/the-woodlands/' | relative_url }}">fencing in The Woodlands</a>.</p>
    <p class="fx-note">Summary only. The recorded standards and your neighborhood's criteria control, and they can change.</p>
