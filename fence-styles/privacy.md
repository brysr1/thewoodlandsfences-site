faq:
  - q: "How tall can my privacy fence be?"
    a: "Depends on your rules: in The Woodlands, 7'6\" solid (8'6\" on major streets); elsewhere usually what your HOA allows, up to 8 feet before a Houston permit is needed."
  - q: "Side-by-side or board-on-board?"
    a: "Side-by-side costs less. As wood dries, small gaps can open between boards. Board-on-board overlaps the pickets, so it stays private as the wood shrinks."
  - q: "Which side faces my neighbor?"
    a: "The finished side faces out, toward the street or the neighbor. In The Woodlands, putting the unfinished side toward a neighbor needs that neighbor's written consent."
  - q: "Cedar or pine?"
    a: "Pine costs less up front. Cedar resists rot and insects better and takes stain well. Both get the same three-rail build and deep posts. See [wood and cedar](/materials/wood-cedar/)."

    {% include eyebrow.html text="Local rules" %}
    <h2>Privacy fence rules in The Woodlands and nearby</h2>
    <p>Section 2.6 of The Woodlands Township's <a href="https://www.thewoodlandstownship-tx.gov/files/sharedassets/main/v/2/covenant-administration/documents/2024-residential-standards-recorded-12-10-2024.pdf" rel="noopener">Residential Development Standards</a> covers privacy fences:</p>
    <ul class="fx-checklist">
      <li><span><b>Approval first.</b> Every new fence needs written approval from your village RDRC.</span></li>
      <li><span><b>Placement.</b> Solid fences stay at least 3 feet behind the front of the house. A rear line along a public street sits 10 feet back from the property line or at the platted building line, whichever is greater.</span></li>
      <li><span><b>Golf and lake lots.</b> No solid fence along the rear line.</span></li>
      <li><span><b>Rot boards.</b> A rot board of 6 inches or less is approvable. Up to 12 inches is allowed if it's screened with evergreen plants from the street.</span></li>
      <li><span><b>Stain.</b> The Standards include a list of approved fence stains.</span></li>
    </ul>
    <p>Outside The Woodlands, your HOA's deed restrictions usually set height, style, and stain color. See <a href="{{ '/service-areas/the-woodlands/' | relative_url }}">fencing in The Woodlands</a>.</p>
    <p class="fx-note">Summary only. The recorded standards and your neighborhood's criteria control, and they can change.</p>
