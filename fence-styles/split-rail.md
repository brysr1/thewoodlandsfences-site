faq_title: Split rail fence questions
faq:
  - q: "Is split rail allowed in The Woodlands?"
    a: "Yes. The Township's construction guidelines show split rail as a transparent fence, so it follows the see-through rules: up to 6'6\" tall, and at least 5 feet back from the front property line with plants in front if it's in the front yard. It needs RDRC approval like any new fence."
  - q: "Can I add wire to keep my dog in?"
    a: "Outside The Woodlands, usually yes, with welded wire behind the rails. In The Woodlands, wire and wire mesh aren't allowed unless they're fully screened inside an approved solid fence, so we'd suggest another option there."
  - q: "Does a rail fence need a permit on acreage?"
    a: "In unincorporated Montgomery and Harris County, a typical residential fence doesn't need a county building permit. If the line crosses a mapped floodplain, a county floodplain permit may apply. We check the flood map for your address."

    <h2>Rail fence rules in The Woodlands and nearby</h2>
    <p>In The Woodlands, Section 2.6 of the Township's <a href="https://www.thewoodlandstownship-tx.gov/files/sharedassets/main/v/2/covenant-administration/documents/2024-residential-standards-recorded-12-10-2024.pdf" rel="noopener">Residential Development Standards</a> applies, and its construction guidelines list split rail as a transparent fence:</p>
    <ul class="fx-checklist">
      <li><span><b>Height.</b> Transparent fences top out at 6'6". Perimeter fences must be at least 4 feet tall.</span></li>
      <li><span><b>Front yards.</b> At least 5 feet back from the front property line, with plants between the fence and the street.</span></li>
      <li><span><b>Wire.</b> Wire and wire mesh aren't allowed unless fully screened inside an approved solid fence. Vinyl isn't allowed either.</span></li>
      <li><span><b>Trees.</b> Fence lines go around trees. A fence can't be fastened to a tree, and trees over 6 inches can't be removed for a fence.</span></li>
    </ul>
    <p>On acreage in Porter, New Caney, Huffman, and unincorporated Montgomery and Harris County, a typical residential fence doesn't need a county building permit, but a mapped floodplain can require a floodplain permit. Splendora is an incorporated city, so we confirm its requirements for addresses inside city limits. See <a href="{{ '/service-areas/porter-new-caney-splendora/' | relative_url }}">Porter, New Caney &amp; Splendora</a> and <a href="{{ '/service-areas/huffman-tx/' | relative_url }}">Huffman</a>.</p>
    <p class="fx-note">Summary only. The recorded standards and your neighborhood's criteria control, and they can change.</p>
