faq_title: Porter, New Caney & Splendora fence questions
faq:
  - q: Do I need a permit for a fence here?
    a: Porter and New Caney are unincorporated Montgomery County, where a typical residential fence doesn't need a county building permit. If the fence is in a mapped floodplain, the county requires a floodplain development permit; three-strand barbed wire is exempt. Splendora is an incorporated city, so we confirm city requirements for addresses inside its limits.
  - q: My builder put in a fence. Can you extend or upgrade it?
    a: Yes. We extend builder fences, add gates, and replace them with board-on-board or a capped design, matching the stain color your HOA specifies.
  - q: Do you build fences for horses?
    a: Yes. We build no-climb wire, board, and pipe fencing, with H-braced corners and wide gates for trailers.
