  - q: Do I need a permit for a fence in New Caney?
    a: New Caney is unincorporated Montgomery County, where a typical residential fence doesn't need a county building permit. If the fence is in a mapped floodplain, the county requires a floodplain development permit, and three-strand barbed wire fences are exempt. We check the flood map for your address.

    a: Yes. We extend builder fences, add gates, and rebuild them in board-on-board or with a cap, matching the stain color your HOA specifies.

    <h2>An old railroad town filling in fast</h2>
    <p>New Caney is an unincorporated community in Montgomery County, north of Kingwood along I-69 (US 59). It was settled in 1862 as Presswood, named for pioneers Austin and Sarah Waters Presswood, with ranches, mills, and stores along Caney Creek. The railroad arrived in 1877, and the post office took the name New Caney in 1882 because another Texas town already used "Caney," according to <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Caney,_Texas" rel="noopener">New Caney's history</a>.</p>
    <p>Today the area mixes older rural lots with large master-planned communities. Most New Caney addresses are in ZIP 77357, and schools are run by New Caney ISD.</p>
    <div class="fx-rule-box">
