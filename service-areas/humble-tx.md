faq_title: Humble fence questions
faq:
  - q: Does the City of Humble require a fence permit?
    a: Yes, for addresses inside city limits. The residential fence permit is $50 plus $1 for every 25 linear feet; commercial fences are $100 plus a per-foot fee. Many "Humble" addresses are actually in unincorporated Harris County, where a fence outside the flood hazard area doesn't need a county permit.
  - q: How do I know if I'm inside city limits?
    a: A Humble mailing address doesn't mean you're in the city. We check your property against city limits and the flood map before we quote.
  - q: Can you replace an older fence on a 1980s lot?
    a: Yes. Neighborhoods like Foxwood and Kenswick were built in the 1980s, and many of their fences are due. We'll tell you whether a repair will hold or a replacement makes more sense.

    <h2>An oil town that became a suburb</h2>
    <p>Humble takes its name from Pleasant Humble, who ran a ferry across the San Jacinto River and became postmaster when the post office opened in 1886. The 1904 oil strike turned it into a boomtown and gave its name to Humble Oil, the company that became Exxon. The city incorporated in 1933 and today is surrounded by unincorporated neighborhoods that share its name.</p>
    <p>That split matters for fencing. Inside city limits, a fence needs a City of Humble permit. Outside, in unincorporated Harris County, a fence outside the special flood hazard area doesn't need a county permit. Either way, the HOA has its own say.</p>
