Before we dig post holes for a new fence, we request utility locates through [Texas 811](https://www.texas811.org/). Utility companies then mark their buried lines in the yard with paint or flags, so we know where not to dig.

## Why it's required

Texas law requires excavators to give notice before they dig. Under [Texas Utilities Code Chapter 251](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/Docs/UT/htm/UT.251.htm), "excavate" covers digging with mechanized equipment 16 inches or deeper, and notice has to go in no earlier than 14 days and no later than 48 hours before the dig, not counting weekends and legal holidays.

That 48-hour window is one reason a fence start date isn't always the next day, even when everything else is ready.

## What gets marked

Member utilities mark the lines they own, such as gas, water, electric, and communications lines.

## What doesn't get marked

Lines you own aren't part of the 811 request. Common ones on a residential lot:

- **Sprinkler lines and valves.**
- **Landscape and pool lighting.**
- **Lines to a detached garage, shed, or gate operator.**
- **Septic lines** on rural lots.

Please show us where yours run before we start, or mark them yourself if you know the layout.

## Before every new install

We request 811 locates before every new install and plan post holes around the marks.
