Most fences in our area don't need a city or county building permit. Many do need approval from a homeowners association or, in The Woodlands, the Township. And a mapped floodplain can add a county permit almost anywhere. Here's how it breaks down.

## The Woodlands

- **Township approval:** nearly every new fence, and any repair over eight linear feet, needs written approval from your village's Residential Design Review Committee under the Township's [Residential Development Standards](https://www.thewoodlandstownship-tx.gov/files/sharedassets/main/v/2/covenant-administration/documents/2024-residential-standards-recorded-12-10-2024.pdf). See our [approval guide](/resources/woodlands-fence-approval-rdrc/).
- **County permit:** most of The Woodlands is in unincorporated Montgomery County, where a floodplain development permit applies only in a mapped floodplain. Creekside Park is in Harris County, where a fence needs a county permit only inside the special flood hazard area.

## Kingwood and other City of Houston addresses

Kingwood has been inside Houston city limits since the end of 1996. Houston requires a fence permit only for fences **over 8 feet** or **masonry and concrete** fences, per the [Houston Permitting Center](https://www.houstonpermittingcenter.org/hpwcode1145). Village deed restrictions apply separately. See [Kingwood](/service-areas/kingwood/).

## City of Humble

Inside Humble city limits, a fence needs a city permit. Many "Humble" mailing addresses are actually in unincorporated Harris County. See [Humble](/service-areas/humble-tx/).

## City of Splendora

The city's [residential permit application](https://cityofsplendora.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/City-of-Splendora-Residential-permit-application.pdf) lists fences as a permit type, so plan on a city permit inside city limits. See [Splendora](/service-areas/splendora-tx/).

## Unincorporated Harris County

Including Atascocita, Huffman, and parts of Spring and Humble. The county's [permit FAQ](https://oce.harriscountytx.gov/Services/Permits/FAQ) says no permit is required for a residential fence outside the special flood hazard area. Inside it, fences count as development, so a permit applies.

## Unincorporated Montgomery County

Including Porter, New Caney, and the parts of Spring north of the county line. A typical residential fence doesn't need a county building permit. Work in a mapped floodplain needs a floodplain development permit, and three-strand barbed wire fences are exempt. See the county's [development regulations](https://cms1files.revize.com/montgomerycountytx/Development%20Regulations%20-%20Adopted%203-4-2025.pdf).

## Everywhere: HOA rules and 811

Outside The Woodlands, your HOA or deed restrictions usually set the rules on height, style, and color. And before every new install, we request [Texas 811](/faq/texas-811/) utility locates.
