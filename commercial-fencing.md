    <h2>Approval and Township standards for commercial sites</h2>
    <p>Commercial property in The Woodlands falls under the Township's <a href="https://www.thewoodlandstownship-tx.gov/files/sharedassets/main/v/2/covenant-administration/documents/commercial-planning-and-design-standards-aug-2026-recorded.pdf" rel="noopener">Commercial Planning and Design Standards</a>. A few points shape almost every job we price there:</p>
    <ul class="fx-checklist">
      <li><span><b>Approval comes first.</b> No wall, screen, or fence may be built unless it's specifically approved, and the Township's <a href="https://www.thewoodlandstownship-tx.gov/Departments/Covenant-Administration/Commercial-Standards" rel="noopener">commercial standards page</a> says exterior improvements are reviewed before construction begins.</span></li>
      <li><span><b>Security fences stay low-key.</b> They must be designed and placed to be as inconspicuous as possible. Chain link with black vinyl-coated fabric, posts, and accessories may be approved case by case.</span></li>
      <li><span><b>Placement.</b> No fence in the street right-of-way, and at least 18 inches between any curb and the fence.</span></li>
      <li><span><b>Enclosures.</b> Dumpster enclosures have to match or complement the building, stand at least 6 inches taller than what they hide, and use metal panel gates with latches.</span></li>
    </ul>
    <p>We read the standards and any recorded site restrictions before we design. Then we prepare the fence plan, materials, and color details for the application, so your submittal goes in complete. Outside The Woodlands, we check city permits and county floodplain rules for your address before we quote. Our <a href="{{ '/industrial-fencing/' | relative_url }}">industrial fencing page</a> lists who regulates what.</p>

    <h2>Gates and access</h2>
    <p>Most problems on a commercial fence show up at the gate. We size each gate to the largest vehicle that uses it and choose the type to fit the drive.</p>
    <ul class="fx-checklist">
      <li><span><b>Swing, slide, or cantilever.</b> Swing gates for standard openings. Slide or cantilever gates for wide openings and tight drives with no room to swing.</span></li>
      <li><span><b>Operators.</b> Solar where there's no power at the gate, wired for heavy gates that cycle all day. Every opener has a manual release.</span></li>
      <li><span><b>Keypads, remotes, and safety sensors.</b> Sensors stop or reverse the gate if a person or vehicle is in the way. People get a separate walk gate.</span></li>
      <li><span><b>Access systems.</b> We don't install card readers or call boxes. We coordinate with your access-control installer so the gate and the system work together.</span></li>
      <li><span><b>Emergency access.</b> We plan fire department access before the gate goes in. In The Woodlands, a gate that restricts vehicle access needs a Knox box or other approved emergency access.</span></li>
    </ul>
    <p>See <a href="{{ '/fence-styles/gate-operators/' | relative_url }}">gate operators</a> for power and safety details.</p>
