      <p>Every fence installation completed by 76 Fence of The Woodlands comes with an <strong>18-month limited workmanship warranty</strong>, starting on the day we finish your project.</p>
      <h2>What's covered</h2>
      <p>Our installation workmanship. If a problem shows up within 18 months that traces back to how our crew built the fence, we'll repair or correct that part at no charge.</p>
      <h2>What isn't covered</h2>
      <ul>
        <li>Acts of God and natural events such as hurricanes, tornadoes, floods, and lightning</li>
        <li>Weather damage, including high wind, heavy rain, and extreme heat or cold</li>
        <li>Normal aging and wear of materials</li>
        <li>Misuse, neglect, or skipped maintenance, and changes or repairs made by others</li>
        <li>Soil movement, including shifting, heaving, settling, and erosion</li>
        <li>Outside impacts, such as vehicles, equipment, or falling trees and limbs</li>
        <li>Roots and plants growing into or over the fence</li>
      </ul>
      <h2>Manufacturer warranties</h2>
      <p>Lumber, metal panels, hardware, and composite boards may carry their own manufacturer warranties. Those are separate from ours, and terms vary by product.</p>
      <h2>Making a claim</h2>
      <p>Call or text <a href="tel:+17133319385">(713) 331-9385</a>, describe what you're seeing, and send clear photos. We'll schedule an inspection, find the cause, and tell you whether it's covered.</p>
