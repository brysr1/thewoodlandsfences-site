Development started in the late 1990s around the 18-hole Augusta Pines Golf Club, a course designed by Tour 18 that hosted a Champions Tour event from 2003 to 2007. Water comes into play on more than a third of its holes.</p>

      <p>So the fence question depends on the lot. A course-side lot usually wants a fence you can see through. A patio home wants a clean, compact privacy fence.
