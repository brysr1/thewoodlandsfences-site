      <h2>Lake, golf course, and big trees</h2>
      <p>Walden on Lake Houston began in 1984 on about 750 acres of piney woods. Lake Houston wraps it on the west and south, and West Lake Houston Parkway runs along the east side. At its center is the Walden on Lake Houston Golf &amp; Country Club, an 18-hole course designed by Robert von Hagge and Bruce Devlin and founded the same year.</p>
      <p>Homes are mostly single-family on large, wooded lots, in sections such as Olympic Village and Sportsman's Village. Many lots face the lake or the course, and some lie in the floodplain. A mandatory HOA maintains the common areas and enforces the deed restrictions.</p>
      <p>That mix sets up the fence decisions. Water and fairway lots want an open back line. Interior lots want privacy. And anything near the shoreline has to handle high water.</p>
