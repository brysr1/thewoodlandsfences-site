      <h2>Where The Woodlands started</h2>
      <p>Grogan's Mill opened on October 19, 1974, as the first village in The Woodlands. It takes its name from the Grogan-Cochran Lumber Company, which owned the land and ran a mill here. The village sits at the south end of The Woodlands, with Town Center to the north and I-45 to the east, in Montgomery County.</p>
      <p>It has about 5,195 homes and roughly 13,500 residents. The Woodlands Resort is here, along with three golf courses, including the resort's Oaks and Panther Trail courses. Parks include Sawmill, Tamarac, and Grogan's Point, and Lake Harrison.</p>
      <p>Fifty years in, a lot of original fences are due for replacement, and the trees have grown up around many fence lines. Golf course lots have their own rule along the back line. Every new fence and any repair over 8 feet goes through the Grogan's Mill Residential Design Review Committee.</p>
