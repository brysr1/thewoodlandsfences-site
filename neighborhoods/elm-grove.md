      <h2>An early Kingwood village on the north side</h2>
      <p>Elm Grove Village sits near West Lake Houston Parkway and Northpark Drive in north Kingwood. It has about 1,418 single-family homes, built out by 1982, and is zoned to Humble ISD, with Elm Grove Elementary in the village. Like the rest of Kingwood, it's in Harris County and inside the City of Houston.</p>
      <p>Elm Grove flooded in 2019. In May of that year, hundreds of homeowners in Elm Grove and North Kingwood Forest sued the builders of Woodridge Village, the tract upstream, saying it had been cleared without detention. Harris County and the city bought that 268-acre site in 2021 for a regional detention facility.</p>
      <p>For a fence here, that history matters. We build with standing water in mind: deeper posts, gaps at the bottom so water can pass, and materials that dry out.</p>
