      <h2>Lakes, trails, and a lot of builder fences</h2>
      <p>Eagle Springs is a master-planned community of about 1,300 acres that Newland began developing in the early 2000s. It sits off Will Clayton Parkway and West Lake Houston Parkway, north of Summerwood, in unincorporated Harris County. Trails link its villages and its man-made lakes, and a 15-acre athletic club anchors the amenities.</p>
      <p>The Eagle Springs Community Association maintains the common areas. Utilities come from three Harris County MUDs: 106, 290, and 419. Schools are Humble ISD.</p>
      <p>Most fence calls here are about the original builder fence, now old enough to lean, gap, and gray. Lake lots and lots on trails often need a different style on the open side.</p>
