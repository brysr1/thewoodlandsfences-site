      <h2>Kingwood's first village</h2>
      <p>Trailwood is Kingwood's oldest village. Its first homes were finished in 1971, the year Kingwood opened as a joint venture of Friendswood Development Company and King Ranch, according to <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingwood,_Texas" rel="noopener">Kingwood's history</a>. <a href="https://communityimpact.com/lake-houston-humble-kingwood/housing-real-estate/get-to-know-the-kingwood-neighborhood-of-trailwood-village-77339/" rel="noopener">Community Impact</a> counts about 790 single-family homes here, in ZIP 77339.</p>
      <p>Lots in Trailwood run larger than in many later villages. <a href="https://har.com/web/jordanms/tools/neighborhoods/detailpt/trailwood/4445" rel="noopener">HAR</a> puts the median lot at about 11,000 square feet and the median home at 1974. Like the rest of Kingwood, Trailwood has been inside the City of Houston since the end of 1996, and the village's deed restrictions apply on top of city rules.</p>
      <p>On a lot this age, the fence has usually been replaced at least once, and the trees have had decades to spread their roots. Both shape how we plan a new fence here.</p>
