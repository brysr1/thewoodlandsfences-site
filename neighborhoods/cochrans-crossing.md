      <h2>Golf, greenbelts, and the middle of town</h2>
      <p>Cochran's Crossing opened in 1983. It's named for the Cochran family of Montgomery County, who co-owned the Grogan-Cochran Lumber Company and much of the land The Woodlands was built on. The village sits in the heart of The Woodlands, around Research Forest Drive, Lake Woodlands Drive, and Cochran's Crossing Drive, in ZIP 77381.</p>
      <p>The Woodlands Country Club is here, along with its Palmer Course, which opened in 1990. So are The Woodlands Recreation Center and Bear Branch and Shadowbend parks.</p>
      <p>For fences, that means a mix of golf course lots, greenbelt lots, and standard interior yards, many with fences from the 1980s and 1990s. Each type has its own limits. The Cochran's Crossing Residential Design Review Committee reviews new fences and larger repairs.</p>
