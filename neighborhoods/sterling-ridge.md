      <h2>Golf lots on the west side</h2>
      <p>Sterling Ridge opened in 1999, the year The Woodlands marked its 25th anniversary, and the name honors that "sterling silver" milestone. It sits west of Kuykendahl Road in ZIP 77382 and was built out by 2005. The George and Cynthia Woods Mitchell Library and the South Montgomery County YMCA are here, along with parks like Cranebrook, Terramont, and Artist Grove.</p>
      <p>Golf is a big part of the village. The Woodlands Country Club's Gary Player course opened here in 2002, and the gated Carlton Woods community has the Jack Nicklaus course, which opened in 2001.</p>
      <p>Homes here went up between 1999 and 2005, so many builder fences are reaching the end of their life. Course lots have a rear-line rule. The Sterling Ridge Residential Design Review Committee reviews fences here.</p>
