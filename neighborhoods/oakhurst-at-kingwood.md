      <h2>A golf course neighborhood in the pines</h2>
      <p>Oakhurst at Kingwood is a Friendswood Development Company community of about 700 acres in the forest on the west side of US 59, in Porter. The centerpiece is Oakhurst Golf Club, an 18-hole daily-fee course designed by Dave Marr that opened in 1994. The community also has neighborhood parks, greenbelts, and a recreation center with a competition-size pool. New home sales finished in 2017.</p>
      <p>Despite the name, Oakhurst sits outside Kingwood and outside Houston city limits, in unincorporated Montgomery County and the 77365 ZIP. The Oakhurst Community Association covers the clubhouse and common areas, and homes are in a MUD. Schools are New Caney ISD.</p>
      <p>Lots run from golf course frontage to pond lots to interior lots under mature trees. That mix, plus a neighborhood that finished building out in 2017, means a lot of our work here is repair and replacement.</p>
