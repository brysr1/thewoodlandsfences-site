      <h2>A neighborhood built inside the trees</h2>
      <p>Spring Trails is a master-planned community in unincorporated Montgomery County, started in 2003 by Houston developer Midway. It sits between the Grand Parkway on the north, Old Holzwarth Road on the west, and the Spring Creek Greenway on the south and east, close to the Hardy Toll Road.</p>
      <p>More than 400 acres of forest were kept as part of the plan, and about 10 miles of hike and bike trails run through it. The community has 19 residential sections, three of them gated, and the Spring Trails Community Association oversees the neighborhood.</p>
      <p>That much forest means a lot of fence lines run along trees, trails, and wooded reserves. Lots near the greenway can also fall in or near a mapped floodplain. We check both before we design.</p>
