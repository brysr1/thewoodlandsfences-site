      <h2>From Arabian horse farm to 2,100 acres of homes</h2>
      <p>Gleannloch Farms started in the 1950s as an Arabian horse farm owned by Douglas Marshall. Homebuilding began in the late 1990s and ran for more than a decade. Today the community covers about 2,100 acres in unincorporated Harris County, roughly bounded by Spring Cypress Road to the south, Champion Forest Drive to the east, and Boudreaux Road to the north.</p>
      <p>The land still shapes the lots. The 27-hole Gleannloch Pines Golf Club opened in 1999, there are several lakes, including catch-and-release fishing lakes, and the equestrian center remains. Homes range from patio homes to larger estates, and some sections are gated. The Gleannloch Farms Community Association oversees the neighborhood.</p>
      <p>For a fence, that means a lot can back onto a fairway, a lake, a trail, or another backyard, and each one calls for a different approach. We look at what's behind your lot, check the flood map, and confirm your section's rules before we price anything.</p>
