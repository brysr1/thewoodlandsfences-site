      <h2>Old trees, old fences, and a nature area next door</h2>
      <p>Bear Branch Village is one of Kingwood's early villages. Its covenants date to 1974, and the median home was built in 1978. It has about 1,091 single-family homes, zoned to Humble ISD with Bear Branch Elementary, Creekwood Middle, and Kingwood High. The entrance to the Creekwood Nature Area, a 50-acre preserve with hiking trails owned by the Kingwood Service Association, is at the end of Maple Park Drive.</p>
      <p>The Bear Branch Village association reviews exterior changes through an architectural committee. Applications go through the TownSq portal and cost nothing to submit. Projects built without approval can be treated as violations.</p>
      <p>Most fence work here is replacing fences that have stood for decades under heavy shade, and setting new posts between roots that have had 50 years to spread.</p>
