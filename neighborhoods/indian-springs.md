      <h2>The hilly village</h2>
      <p>Indian Springs opened in 1984. It's named for Native American artifacts found on the land, tied to the Bidai people, and construction near the creek that year turned up some of them. It's the smallest village in The Woodlands and the only one with real hills. It sits near Woodlands Parkway, mostly in ZIP 77381 with a small part in 77382.</p>
      <p>The village has eight parks, including Forestgate and Falconwing with their pools, and pond parks like Deer Rush Pond and Reedy Pond.</p>
      <p>Grade is the big fence issue here. Slopes change how panels are set, how posts are footed, and how water moves along the fence line. Indian Springs is also the one village with two review committees, so we confirm which one covers your lot.</p>
