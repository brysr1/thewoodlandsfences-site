      <h2>The Woodlands, south of Spring Creek</h2>
      <p>Creekside Park opened in October 2007 and is named for Spring Creek, which separates it from the older villages. It's the only village in Harris County, with addresses in 77375 and 77389. At build-out it covers about 3,500 acres and roughly 7,100 homes. It's also the only village with a village green.</p>
      <p>The village includes the George Mitchell Nature Preserve, the Rob Fleming Aquatic Center, part of the Spring Creek Greenway, and lake and pond parks like Lake Paloma, Lake Voyager, and Hullwood Pond. The gated Carlton Woods Creekside has the Tom Fazio course, which opened in 2005.</p>
      <p>Most homes are newer, so many fences here are builder fences ready for an upgrade or their first replacement. Fences still go through the Creekside Park Residential Design Review Committee, the same as any other village. Harris County only affects the permit check.</p>
