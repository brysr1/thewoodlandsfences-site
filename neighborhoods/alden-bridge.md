      <h2>A big village next to the state forest</h2>
      <p>Alden Bridge opened in 1994. Roger Galatas, then president of The Woodlands Operating Company, named it after his hometown of Alden Bridge, Louisiana. It's one of the largest villages in The Woodlands, with about 7,000 homes and more than 18,000 residents, near FM 2978 and FM 1488 and next to W.G. Jones State Forest.</p>
      <p>The village has about 25 parks and 13 aquatic facilities. Lakeside Park, on West Alden Bridge Drive, sits beside Bear Branch Reservoir, one of the largest bodies of water in The Woodlands, and Windvale Park has a fishing pond.</p>
      <p>Most fence work here is family-yard work: pet fences, pool barriers, and replacing fences from the 1990s and 2000s. Lots on the reservoir or a pond may fall under the rear-line rule for lake lots. The Alden Bridge Residential Design Review Committee handles approvals.</p>
