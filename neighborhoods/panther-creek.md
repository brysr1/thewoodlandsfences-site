      <h2>A lake village from the late 1970s</h2>
      <p>Panther Creek opened in 1977 as the second village in The Woodlands. It's named for the creek that widens into Lake Woodlands, which forms the village's eastern edge. Northshore and Southshore parks sit on the lake, and the village has about 3,600 homes and more than 12,000 residents in ZIP 77381.</p>
      <p>That gives Panther Creek two kinds of fence jobs. Lake lots fall under the Township rule that bans solid fences on the rear line, so the back usually gets see-through steel. Inland, many homes still have fences from the original build-out that are ready to be replaced.</p>
      <p>New fences and larger repairs go to the Panther Creek Residential Design Review Committee, which meets monthly at Township Town Hall.</p>
