faq:
  - q: Does a repair need approval in The Woodlands?
    a: Under the Township's Residential Development Standards, a repair needs approval when more than 8 linear feet is replaced, redesigned, rebuilt, or relocated. Smaller fixes generally don't.
  - q: Can you price a repair from photos?
    a: Often, yes. Text a few photos and a short description to (713) 331-9385. Larger or unclear repairs get a site visit.
  - q: Repair or replace?
    a: If a few posts or sections are bad and the rest is sound, repair. If posts are failing along much of the line or rot is widespread, replacement usually costs less over time. We'll tell you which.
  - q: Can you match the rest of my fence?
    a: Usually. We rebuild damaged sections in the same style and height as what's standing. New wood looks lighter than weathered boards at first, and staining the whole run evens it out.
  - q: Do you repair automatic gate openers?
    a: Yes. We install and repair automatic gate openers as well as the gates they move. See [gates](/fence-styles/gates/).
  - q: Is a repair covered by your warranty?
    a: Only runs we fully build ourselves. New pickets on someone else's existing rails or posts aren't covered.
  - q: Does my pool fence need a new inspection after a repair?
    a: In The Woodlands, changing more than eight feet of a fence that serves as a pool barrier, or installing or repairing its gates, triggers a new inspection.

    <h2>Approval for repairs in The Woodlands and HOAs</h2>
    <p>Small repairs in The Woodlands usually don't need an application. Larger ones do. Under the Township's <a href="https://www.thewoodlandstownship-tx.gov/files/sharedassets/main/v/2/covenant-administration/documents/2024-residential-standards-recorded-12-10-2024.pdf" rel="noopener">Residential Development Standards</a>:</p>
    <ul class="fx-checklist">
      <li><span><b>Over 8 linear feet needs approval.</b> When more than eight feet of fence is replaced, redesigned, reconstructed, or relocated, you need written approval from your village RDRC through the Civic Access Portal.</span></li>
      <li><span><b>Pool barriers.</b> Changing more than eight feet of a fence that serves as a pool barrier, or installing or repairing its gates, triggers a new inspection.</span></li>
      <li><span><b>Good-neighbor fences.</b> A new alternating-side fence needs your neighbor's written consent, but repairing or replacing an existing one doesn't.</span></li>
      <li><span><b>Stain.</b> Transparent and semi-transparent stains on the Township's list are pre-approved. Paint and solid-color stains need approval first.</span></li>
    </ul>
    <p>Our <a href="{{ '/resources/woodlands-fence-approval-rdrc/' | relative_url }}">step-by-step approval guide</a> covers what to submit. Outside The Woodlands, your HOA's deed restrictions usually set the style, height, and stain color, and a rebuilt section should match them.</p>
    <p class="fx-note">Summary only. The recorded standards and your neighborhood's criteria control, and they can change.</p>
