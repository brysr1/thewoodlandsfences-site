    <h2>Before you build</h2>
    <div class="fx-cards">
      <div class="fx-card"><h3>Find your rules first</h3><p>Check three things: your jurisdiction (The Woodlands, Houston, Humble, or the county), your flood zone, and your HOA or deed restrictions. All three can change what you're allowed to build. Our <a href="{{ '/service-areas/' | relative_url }}">service area pages</a> summarize each.</p></div>
      <div class="fx-card"><h3>Know your property line</h3><p>A survey shows where your lot ends and any easements. Fences in easements are at your risk, and in The Woodlands they need the easement holder's approval.</p></div>
      <div class="fx-card"><h3>Plan around trees</h3><p>Route the fence around trunks and give roots room. Never fasten a fence to a tree. In The Woodlands, trees over 6 inches can't be removed for a fence.</p></div>
      <div class="fx-card"><h3>Why posts fail</h3><p>Most fences fail at the posts: too shallow, too little concrete, or set in wet ground without drainage. Ask any contractor how deep and how much concrete.</p><a class="more" href="{{ '/pricing-guide/' | relative_url }}">What goes into ours &rarr;</a></div>
      <div class="fx-card"><h3>Storm prep and damage</h3><p>Before hurricane season, fix loose boards and leaning posts, and trim limbs over the fence. After a storm, photograph damage before cleanup for insurance.</p><a class="more" href="{{ '/maintenance-repair/' | relative_url }}">Repairs &rarr;</a></div>
      <div class="fx-card"><h3>Keep wood looking good</h3><p>Aim sprinklers away from the fence, keep soil and mulch off the boards, and restain every few years.</p><a class="more" href="{{ '/fence-staining-power-washing/' | relative_url }}">Staining &rarr;</a></div>
    </div>
