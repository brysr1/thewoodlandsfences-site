    <p><strong>Last updated:</strong> October 19, 2026</p>
    <p>This policy covers information 76 Fence of The Woodlands ("we," "us") receives through thewoodlandsfences.com, how we use and share it, and your choices.</p>

    </ul>

    <h2>Services we use</h2>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Formspree</strong> delivers contact form messages to us.</li>
      <li><strong>Jobber</strong> hosts our online booking; it handles what you enter there on our behalf.</li>
      <li><strong>Google Maps</strong> embeds may set cookies under Google's privacy policy.</li>
      <li><strong>Hearth</strong> receives your information if you apply for financing, under its own terms.</li>
    </ul>

    <h2>Sharing</h2>
    <p>We don't sell personal information. We share it only with the providers above as needed to run the site and business, with other 76 Fence companies when your project requires it, at your request, or when the law requires.</p>

    <h2>Cookies and choices</h2>
