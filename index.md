faq_title: Common questions
faq:
  - q: Do I need approval to build a fence in The Woodlands?
    a: Yes. The Woodlands Residential Development Standards require prior written approval for any new fence, and for repairs that replace or move more than 8 linear feet. Applications go to your village's Residential Design Review Committee through the Township's online portal. We prepare the drawing and material details for you. See [The Woodlands](/service-areas/the-woodlands/) for the main rules.
  - q: How tall can my fence be?
    a: In The Woodlands, solid fences are generally capped at 7'6", or 8'6" along major roads, and see-through fences at 6'6". In unincorporated Harris County and in Kingwood (City of Houston), the bigger questions are your deed restrictions and, in Houston, whether the fence goes over 8 feet, which needs a city permit.
  - q: Do I need a permit in Spring, Atascocita, or Huffman?
    a: In unincorporated Harris County, a residential fence outside the special flood hazard area doesn't need a county permit. Inside a mapped floodplain it counts as development and does. We check the flood map for your lot before we quote.
  - q: Do you call 811 before digging?
    a: Every time. Utility lines get marked before any post hole is dug.
