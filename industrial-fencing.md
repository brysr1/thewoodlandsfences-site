faq_title: Industrial fencing questions
faq:
  - q: Do I need a permit for an industrial fence?
    a: "It depends on where the site is. Inside Houston city limits, a fence permit is needed for fences over 8 feet or masonry and concrete fences. In unincorporated Harris County, a fence needs a county permit inside the special flood hazard area, and in unincorporated Montgomery County, work in a mapped floodplain needs a floodplain development permit. Commercial sites in The Woodlands need approval under the Township's commercial standards. We check before we bid."
  - q: Can you keep my site secure while you replace the fence?
    a: "Yes. We phase the work so sections come out and go back in on a schedule built around your operations, and we talk through any openings before the old fence comes down."
  - q: Do you install card readers or call boxes on gates?
    a: "No. We build the gates and install operators with keypads and remotes. For card readers, call boxes, or a full access-control system, we coordinate with your access-control installer."

    <h2>Who regulates an industrial fence</h2>
    <div class="fx-rule-box">
      <p><strong>City of Houston</strong> (including Kingwood and parts of the I-69 corridor): a fence permit is needed for fences over 8 feet or masonry and concrete fences, per the <a href="https://www.houstonpermittingcenter.org/hpwcode1145" rel="noopener">Houston Permitting Center</a>.</p>
      <p><strong>Unincorporated Harris County:</strong> a fence needs a county permit inside the special flood hazard area. See the Harris County Engineering <a href="https://oce.harriscountytx.gov/Services/Permits/FAQ" rel="noopener">permit FAQ</a>.</p>
      <p><strong>Unincorporated Montgomery County:</strong> work in a mapped floodplain needs a floodplain development permit. See the county's <a href="https://cms1files.revize.com/montgomerycountytx/Development%20Regulations%20-%20Adopted%203-4-2025.pdf" rel="noopener">development regulations</a>.</p>
      <p><strong>The Woodlands:</strong> commercial fences need approval before construction, and security fences must be as inconspicuous as possible. Black vinyl-coated chain link may be approved case by case. See our <a href="{{ '/resources/commercial-security-fencing-woodlands-tx/' | relative_url }}">Woodlands commercial guide</a>.</p>

    <h2>Gates that keep trucks moving</h2>
    <p>On an industrial site, the gate gets more use than any other part of the fence. We size gates to the trucks and equipment that use them, choose swing, slide, or cantilever to fit the drive, and install operators with monitored safety sensors, keypads, and remotes. People get a separate walk gate rather than sharing the vehicle gate.</p>
    <p>We don't install card readers or call boxes. If your site uses them, we coordinate with your access-control installer. Plan fire department access before a gate goes in. See <a href="{{ '/fence-styles/gate-operators/' | relative_url }}">gate operators</a> for the safety and power details.</p>
