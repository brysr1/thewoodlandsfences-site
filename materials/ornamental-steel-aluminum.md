      <h2>Security without blocking the view</h2>
      <p>Ornamental metal is the answer wherever a solid fence isn't allowed or isn't wanted. In The Woodlands, solid fences aren't permitted along the rear line of golf course and lake lots, and see-through fences can go to 6'6". Many HOAs in Spring, Kingwood, and Atascocita set similar rules for water and fairway lots.</p>
      <p>Around Houston, ornamental fencing almost always means powder-coated steel. It's what local supply houses stock, it's heavier and stronger than aluminum, and it works for everything from pool barriers and backyards to drive gates and security fencing. Aluminum is common in other parts of the country, but supply houses here don't stock it, so it has to be brought in.</p>
