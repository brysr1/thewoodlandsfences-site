      <h2>Wood look, little upkeep</h2>
      <p>Composite fencing combines wood fiber and plastic into boards that won't rot, warp, or need stain. Trex is the best-known brand. It costs more up front and pays it back in years without staining.</p>
      <p>Because it contains plastic, composite may not be allowed under every set of rules, including The Woodlands standards, which prohibit plastic fencing. We check before you choose it.</p>
      <ul class="fx-checklist">
        <li><span><b>No staining</b> and no rot.</span></li>
