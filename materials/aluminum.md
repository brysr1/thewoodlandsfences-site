faq_title: Aluminum fence questions
faq:
  - q: "Aluminum or steel?"
    a: "Both look like black wrought iron from the street. Aluminum can't rust and is lighter, so it's a good fit near pools, water, and damp shaded yards. Steel is heavier and stiffer, so it's the stronger choice for drive gates and security. Many yards use both. See [ornamental steel](/materials/ornamental-steel-aluminum/)."
  - q: "Is aluminum allowed in The Woodlands?"
    a: "Yes. The Township's standards allow metal fences, and aluminum counts as a see-through fence, up to 6'6\" tall. Like any new fence, it needs RDRC approval first."
  - q: "Will an aluminum fence keep my dog in?"
    a: "For most dogs, yes. Small dogs can slip through standard picket spacing, so ask about puppy pickets. See [pet fences](/fence-styles/pet/)."
  - q: "Does aluminum need painting?"
    a: "Not as routine upkeep. The factory powder coat holds up well in strong sun, and there's no steel underneath to rust if it gets scratched. A rinse now and then keeps it clean."

      <h2>The wrought iron look, without the rust</h2>
      <p>From the street, an aluminum fence looks like black wrought iron: pickets between rails, with a flat or spear top. Underneath the powder coat there's no iron to rust, so a scratch from a mower or a dragged pool chair doesn't turn into an orange streak.</p>
      <p>It's lighter than steel, which makes it easy to rack along a sloped yard, and it keeps the view open on the golf course, lake, and reserve lots where The Woodlands doesn't allow a solid fence on the back line.</p>
      <ul class="fx-checklist">
        <li><span><b>Can't rust</b>, even where the finish is scratched.</span></li>

    <h2>Aluminum fence rules in The Woodlands and nearby</h2>
    <p>Section 2.6 of The Woodlands Township's <a href="https://www.thewoodlandstownship-tx.gov/files/sharedassets/main/v/2/covenant-administration/documents/2024-residential-standards-recorded-12-10-2024.pdf" rel="noopener">Residential Development Standards</a> allows metal fences. For aluminum:</p>
    <ul class="fx-checklist">
      <li><span><b>Height.</b> See-through fences top out at 6'6".</span></li>
      <li><span><b>Golf course and lake lots.</b> Solid fences aren't allowed along the rear line, so an open metal fence is the usual choice.</span></li>
      <li><span><b>Front yards.</b> A see-through fence in a front yard has to sit at least 5 feet back from the front property line, with plants between it and the street.</span></li>
      <li><span><b>Pools.</b> Pool barrier fences and gates need approval and a code inspection.</span></li>
    </ul>
    <p>Outside The Woodlands, your HOA's deed restrictions usually set the style and color, and lake and golf communities often require black ornamental metal on the back line. See <a href="{{ '/service-areas/the-woodlands/' | relative_url }}">fencing in The Woodlands</a>.</p>
    <p class="fx-note">Summary only. The recorded standards and your neighborhood's criteria control, and they can change.</p>
