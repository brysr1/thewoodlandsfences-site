  - q: "Can I put chain link in my backyard?"
    a: "Depends on your rules. It's prohibited in The Woodlands unless fully screened, and restricted by many HOAs. On acreage and in unrestricted areas, it's common."
  - q: "Galvanized or black vinyl-coated?"
    a: "Galvanized costs less and is fine for utility areas and commercial yards. Black vinyl-coated costs more but blends into trees and landscaping, so it's the usual pick where people will see it."
  - q: "Can chain link be a privacy fence?"
    a: "Partly. Privacy slats woven into the fabric block most of the view. If full privacy is the goal, a wood fence usually looks better for about the same effort. See [privacy fences](/fence-styles/privacy/)."
  - q: "Will chain link keep my dog in?"
    a: "For most dogs, yes. For diggers, we add a dig barrier along the base. For jumpers, go taller. See [pet fences](/fence-styles/pet/)."

    <h2>Chain link rules in The Woodlands and nearby</h2>
    <p>Section 2.6 of The Woodlands Township's <a href="https://www.thewoodlandstownship-tx.gov/files/sharedassets/main/v/2/covenant-administration/documents/2024-residential-standards-recorded-12-10-2024.pdf" rel="noopener">Residential Development Standards</a> is strict about chain link:</p>
    <ul class="fx-checklist">
      <li><span><b>Prohibited unless screened.</b> Chain link, wire, and wire mesh are only allowed fully screened from view inside an approved solid fence.</span></li>
      <li><span><b>Dog runs.</b> Animal runs are covered separately, in Section 2.4 of the Standards.</span></li>
      <li><span><b>Posts.</b> Metal posts are limited to 2-inch galvanized posts on the inside of a solid fence, or the posts of a wrought iron fence.</span></li>
    </ul>
    <p>On acreage in unincorporated Montgomery and Harris County, a typical residential fence doesn't need a county building permit, though a mapped floodplain can require a floodplain permit. Outside The Woodlands, many HOAs restrict chain link to backyards or ban it, so we check your deed restrictions first. See <a href="{{ '/service-areas/the-woodlands/' | relative_url }}">fencing in The Woodlands</a>.</p>
    <p class="fx-note">Summary only. The recorded standards and your neighborhood's criteria control, and they can change.</p>
