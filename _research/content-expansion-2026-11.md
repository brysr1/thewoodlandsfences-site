## Township rules pages (pool, materials, repair approval, stain colors)
- The Woodlands Township Residential Development Standards 2024, recorded 12/10/2024:
  https://www.thewoodlandstownship-tx.gov/files/sharedassets/main/v/2/covenant-administration/documents/2024-residential-standards-recorded-12-10-2024.pdf
  - 1.5 (p. 11): approval when >8 linear ft replaced/redesigned/reconstructed/relocated; any security-barrier gate installed/replaced/etc.; minor-repair exemption "does not include fencing, roofing and color changes".
  - 1.6: chain link, hog wire, wire mesh, wire-bound pickets unacceptable; metal posts only 2" galvanized or in wrought iron; gloss paint prohibited.
  - 2.6 C: good-neighbor consent waived for repair/replacement of an existing one. 2.6 F/J: black mesh rules. 2.6 J (p. 44): allowed/prohibited materials, "straight and plumb". 2.6 L (p. 45): stain/paint.
  - 2.13 (pp. 64-67): barrier for pools/spas/hot tubs/ponds >2 ft; IRC; third-party inspection; re-inspection triggers; temporary barrier; 1/1/1986 neighbor-fence condition; shared fence warning; 4-ft construction barricade; above-ground pool 6-ft solid screen; pneumatic enclosures not acceptable; equipment screening.
  - Appendix C (p. 89): stains transparent/semi-transparent unless approved; neighborhood character and street visibility; screening case by case.
