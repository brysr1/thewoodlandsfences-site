faq:
  - q: Which stains are allowed in The Woodlands?
    a: Under the Township's standards, wood may weather naturally, and transparent and semi-transparent stains on the Township's list are pre-approved. Paint and solid-color stains need approval first.
  - q: How soon can a new fence be stained?
    a: New wood needs time to dry before it takes stain well. We'll tell you when your fence is ready.
  - q: How often should I restain?
    a: It depends on sun, shade, and sprinklers. Many fences here benefit from a fresh coat every few years.
